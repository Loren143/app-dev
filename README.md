# My Favorite Story: The Adventures of Tom Sawyer

> *"Work consists of whatever a body is obliged to do, and that Play consists of whatever a body is not obliged to do."* — Mark Twain

**The Adventures of Tom Sawyer** is a classic 1876 novel by *Mark Twain* about a young boy growing up along the Mississippi River. Set in the fictional town of St. Petersburg, Missouri, the story follows Tom's mischievous escapades, childhood friendships, and thrilling encounters with real danger.

## Why I Love It
* **Relatable humor:** Tom's clever tricks—like convincing his friends to whitewash Aunt Polly's fence for him—are timeless.
* **Sense of adventure:** Exploring McDougal's Cave and hunting for buried treasure captures the spirit of childhood freedom.
* **Memorable friendship:** The bond between Tom and Huckleberry Finn drives the heart of the story.

## Top 3 Iconic Moments
1. **The Fence Painting Trick** – Turning a boring chore into a profitable trade.
2. **Jackson's Island Escapade** – Running away to become "pirates" and sneaking back to watch their own funeral.
3. **The Treasure in the Cave** – Outsmarting Injun Joe and finding the hidden gold.
