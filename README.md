# The Adventures of Tom Sawyer

This is one of my favorite cartoons. It's about a boy named Tom Sawyer who loves going on adventures with his friends, especially Huck Finn.

## Why I Like It

I like this cartoon because it's fun to watch. Tom is also funny and always gets into different situations.

## Characters I Like

- **Tom Sawyer** - The main character.
- **Huckleberry Finn** - Tom's best friend.
- **Becky Thatcher** - Tom's friend.

## Favorite Moments

1. Tom painting Aunt Polly's fence.
2. Tom and his friends going to Jackson's Island.
3. Tom and Huck finding hidden treasure.

## About the Story

- **Author:** Mark Twain
- **Genre:** Adventure
- **Main Character:** Tom Sawyer
